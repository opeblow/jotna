# Privacy policy

Jotna is designed to minimize sensitive data collection and to avoid holding customer funds or private keys.

## Data we may process

- Seller profile information needed to create and manage an order flow
- Order metadata such as price, customer label, timestamps, and delivery status
- Wallet connection identifiers required for Lightning invoice operations
- Uploads needed to create product listings or order images

## What we do not do

- We do not custody user funds.
- We do not store buyer private keys.
- We do not treat wallet secrets as application logs.

## Security controls

- Wallet hosts are validated before connection.
- Secrets are kept out of logs.
- Cookie-based sessions are scoped to the app origin.
- Sensitive operations are rate-limited and protected by request validation.

## Retention

Order records and wallet metadata are retained only as long as required to provide the service and support operational integrity.

## Contact

For privacy questions, email privacy@jotna.app or open a repository discussion.
